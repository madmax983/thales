# 🌟 Nova: Market Temperature

## 💡 The Spark
"I noticed we have gravity and seismology, but what about the raw 'heat' of the market—combining volume and rate of change into a single metric?"

## 🚀 The Feature
"Implemented `analyze_temperature` in `crates/cli/src/experimental/market_temperature.rs`, exposed via `thales-cli analyze-temperature`."

## 🔮 The Potential
"Could be used to detect overheating markets before a blow-off top or freezing markets right before an explosive move."

## ⚠️ Risk
"Low. Isolated in `src/experimental/market_temperature.rs` and behind the `nova` feature flag."
